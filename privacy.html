Privacy Policy

Effective March 22, 2026.

What We Collect

Third String Shirts is a lightweight storefront. We do not offer user accounts on this site, and we do not ask you to create a profile just to browse.

We may receive information you choose to send us directly, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message when you contact us at hello@thirdstringshirts.com.

Like most websites, our hosting or platform providers may also collect basic technical information automatically, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and page request logs needed to serve and secure the site.

Orders and Checkout

This site links to third-party selling platforms, including Spreadshirt and Etsy, for product pages, checkout, payment, shipping, and related order processing.

When you place an order through one of those platforms, your payment details, shipping details, and order information are collected by that platform under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not directly process credit card payments on this website.

How We Use Information

We use information we receive to respond to messages, help with customer support requests, review store issues, and keep the site running.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help host the site, route email, or operate our storefront, but only as needed to run the business.

Retention

We keep messages and support-related information only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, troubleshooting, recordkeeping, or legal compliance.

Your Options

You can contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information we directly control. For marketplace or checkout information held by Spreadshirt or Etsy, you may also need to contact those platforms directly.
